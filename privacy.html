Privacy policy

rclone-drive is a personal OAuth client used only by its owner to access their own Google Drive through rclone on their own computer.

  • Google Drive data is transferred only between Google and the owner's computer.
  • No data is collected, stored on any server, shared, or sold.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only in the owner's local rclone configuration and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
